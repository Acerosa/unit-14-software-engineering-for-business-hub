# Week 2 activities

Week 2 is authored as one canonical `lp.content` graph and consumed through the same renderer as Week 1.

Teaching copy follows the Unit 14 Scheme of Learning sequence for **Data Type Conversion and Predefined Subroutines** (LO1, Assignment 1, P1). The SoL `.docx` was not present in this environment; content follows the Week 2 sequence supplied for this hub. It does not invent Week 3 selection teaching.

The import artefact is `content/packages/week-2.json`. After review it is merged into `content/unit-14/`.
